EO Token Authority Policy Correction

Corrected policy:
- Mint Authority: permanently revoked after initial issuance.
- Freeze Authority: retained under a controlled policy.
- Intended uses include pre-disclosed lock-up/vesting of initial allocations and Team & Founders allocations, plus exceptional legal, regulatory or security requirements.
- Ordinary user accounts are not intended to be frozen arbitrarily for operational convenience.

This patch replaces the prior wording that stated Freeze Authority would be permanently revoked.
