TOKEN ECONOMY

Fixed Supply, Staged Distribution

Maximum supply is fixed at 5,000,000,000 EO. After initial issuance, the Mint Authority is intended to be permanently revoked. The Freeze Authority is retained under a controlled policy for pre-disclosed lock-up/vesting and exceptional legal, regulatory or security requirements; ordinary user accounts are not intended to be frozen arbitrarily.
