EO Foundation, Treasury & Governance

PUBLIC RELEASE · ENGLISH EDITION

EO Foundation Structure

The baseline direction is to establish EO Foundation as an overseas nonprofit-type foundation before TGE. The Foundation is intended to oversee the EO Token ecosystem, Treasury, Tokenomics, ecosystem policy and Governance, with a role separated from EO Global's commercial activities.

Jurisdiction Selection

The jurisdiction will be selected through a combined assessment of digital-asset regulatory clarity, international credibility, compatibility with global exchanges and financial institutions, formation and operating costs, tax structure, and the practical operation of Treasury and Governance.

Board & Advisory

Initial structure: a small board centered on founders and core operators.

Legal, finance, blockchain, tax and business advisers may be added as needed.

Holding EO Token itself does not confer Foundation decision-making, management or voting rights.

Foundation-centered operation remains the long-term baseline; a DAO is not currently planned.

EO Foundation, Treasury & Governance

Routine Treasury execution may be performed by the operating team within approved policies and budgets. Transactions that may materially affect the ecosystem - including large asset movements, strategic investments, major partnerships and use of the Strategic Reserve - are subject to separate EO Foundation board approval.

Treasury Deployment Priority

1. Initial: EO Wallet, token infrastructure, EO Shopping Mall, service integration, legal and operating systems.

2. Growth: Content and data, utility expansion and external partnerships.

3. Expansion: Global market entry and support for new businesses and services.

Transparency

Principle of disclosing core wallet addresses related to Token Treasury and major supply / burn flows.

Quarterly Treasury reporting.

Category-level disclosure for technology, Shopping Mall, content, marketing, legal, rewards and other major uses.

Separate notice for large transactions with material market or ecosystem impact.

Treasury EO conversions into fiat or stablecoins are limited to actual business-operating purposes. Conversions above a material threshold are subject to a separate approval procedure, with the specific threshold to be defined later in Governance policy.
