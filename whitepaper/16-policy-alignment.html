Policy Alignment

PUBLIC RELEASE · ENGLISH EDITION

Some mid- and long-term EO ecosystem concepts may intersect with policy directions considered or pursued by governments in areas such as peaceful exchange, public-health cooperation, peaceful use of the DMZ, peace and unification education, and broader use of North Korea-related materials. Such policy intersections do not mean that the EO project has government approval, support, guarantees or a confirmed joint project.
