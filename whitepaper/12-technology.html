Technical Paper Separation

The official whitepaper describes only the high-level technical architecture. Detailed implementation topics - including wallet creation and recovery, local key storage, payment verification, price quotes, refund reserves, operational wallets, incident response and Mainnet validation - are managed separately in the EO Technical Paper.

Deployment Principle

EO Token and EO Wallet are intended to undergo internal validation and staged deployment, with service stability verified in the Mainnet environment before broad release to general users. Test periods, deployment procedures and incident response are managed in the EO Technical Paper and internal operating policies.
