Compliance & Risk

PUBLIC RELEASE · ENGLISH EDITION

Utility Token Design Intent

EO Token is designed as a utility token for service use, payments, Experience & Access Utility and information-product purchases within the EO ecosystem. It is not intended to provide company equity, dividend rights, interest income, principal protection or a specific investment return.

Final legal classification and regulatory treatment may differ depending on the countries of sale and distribution, service structure and applicable laws of the relevant jurisdiction. Final determinations will be made only after required legal and regulatory review before issuance, sale, listing and service operation.

Risk-Based KYC / AML

Rather than applying KYC uniformly to every token holder, the EO ecosystem adopts a risk-based direction in which KYC/AML is applied to the Pre-Sale, fiat-linked services, selected regulated services and transactions where required by law. Users located in sanctioned or restricted jurisdictions, sanctioned individuals or entities, and users for whom service is legally restricted may be excluded from the Pre-Sale and selected official services.

Change Policy

Legal and regulatory review before TGE may lead to changes in the current Pre-Sale method, KYC/AML, Foundation structure, selected Utilities and Tokenomics. Material changes to Tokenomics, Foundation, Roadmap or Compliance are managed through a new whitepaper version, while minor changes may be handled through official notices.

Conditional Expansion Compliance

Long-term North Korea-related visions are explained while clearly marking each area as CONDITIONAL or R&D. Before actual entry into any such business, project-specific legal, regulatory, sanctions and licensing review is performed again.

Public Disclosure Boundaries

North Korea-related movement of personnel, employment or settlement structures are not treated as executable models until applicable legal, sanctions and approval reviews are complete.

Inheritance, remittance and profit-distribution structures are not presented as official business models; related areas are described primarily through non-financial counseling and information services.

Tourism infrastructure assumes lawful tourism, accommodation and cultural facilities within applicable law and licensing; unconfirmed facility types are not presented as confirmed projects.

OTT platforms, government agencies or partners are not named as official partners or official linkages unless an MOU, contract or other relationship has been objectively verified.

Financial projections that could encourage investment decisions - including business-revenue forecasts, FDV, ROI or target price - are excluded from the official whitepaper.

Specific artists, rights holders, performance dates, regions, routes or MOUs related to EO Express are not presented as confirmed partners or schedules before official agreement.

Specific costs, success fees, large investment effects, remittances or funeral-related profit sharing in humanitarian and family-support projects are excluded from public-whitepaper financial forecasts and execution promises.

Partner Disclosure

Only partners with objectively verifiable relationships such as contracts, MOUs or formal agreements are disclosed by name. Organizations at the proposal, discussion or contact stage are not presented as official partners.
