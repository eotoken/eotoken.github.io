Experience & Access Utility

PUBLIC RELEASE · ENGLISH EDITION

One of EO Token's differentiated initial utilities is special access to performances, broadcasts and events. Separate from Shopping Mall payments, EO Token is intended to connect users with limited experiences and participation opportunities that may be difficult to obtain through ordinary payment alone.

Core Models

ModelDescription
Basic applicationUsers meeting event-specific EO holding conditions may receive a basic application opportunity.
Additional applicationsEO Token may be used for additional applications. EO used for an application is not returned regardless of selection outcome.
EO special seatingDepending on the event, full EO payment, EO application or another suitable model may be used.
Premium experienceArtist-linked special viewing environments, broadcast audience access and limited programs.

EO does not currently adopt a fixed Basic / Silver / Gold tier system. Holding conditions, application amounts and EO-use requirements are designed separately for each concert, broadcast or event.

Event Token Flow

EO Token used for paid applications may, depending on operating policy, be transferred to Treasury, recirculated through the ecosystem or partially burned. Detailed allocation criteria and any recurring burn program would be introduced only after sufficient real-use data has accumulated and after prior notice.

EO Express — Global Experience IP Platform

EO Express is a composite Experience IP platform concept intended to connect the movement, on-site documentation, live distribution, documentary production, publishing and tourism surrounding an event into a long-term IP value chain rather than ending with a one-off performance. Its core vision is “One Event → One Journey → One Global IP.” Specific artists, dates, routes, broadcasters, OTT platforms or contracts are not presented as confirmed until official agreements have been verified.

One Event → One Journey → One Global IP

The focus of EO Express is not only the performance itself, but the conversion of the entire surrounding process into content, experience and tourism IP. The conceptual model progressively links an event, curated travel, field documentation, Live / OTT, documentary, publishing and tourism. Execution at each stage depends on rights clearance, partner agreements, local regulation and operating conditions.

Six IP Portfolio Axes

EO Express is designed so that one event may expand across multiple business and IP axes, including OTT distribution, Online Live, Documentary, Publishing, Tourism and Sponsorship. These are not guaranteed revenue streams; each is an optional portfolio path that may be executed only after required rights, partner contracts and regulatory review are secured.

Gate-Based Execution

Actual EO Express execution proceeds only when prerequisite gates are satisfied, including official approval of the performance or cultural event, international-sanctions review, negotiations with artists and rights holders, content-distribution rights, and safety, insurance, entry/exit and transportation conditions. No specific artist or timing is presented in this whitepaper as a confirmed partner or schedule.

EO Token Connection

In the future, EO Token may be connected to EO Express special seating, event applications, limited programs, content access and tourism / experience services. Any such connection remains within the existing Experience & Access Utility principles, with actual conditions disclosed before the relevant event or service launch.
