Content & Intelligence Utility

PUBLIC RELEASE · ENGLISH EDITION

B2B Intelligence Model

North Korea NOW and the regional database are intended to expand beyond free content into information products for companies and institutions. Basic information remains open, while in-depth reports and structured data may be monetized to support a sustainable data business.

Revenue TypeDescriptionPayment Principle
Report SalesSingle-sale standard reportsFiat or EO
SubscriptionMonthly / annual data and report subscriptionsFiat or EO
EO ExclusiveSelected EO-only reports, data packs and early-access analysisDirect EO payment
Custom ResearchCustomized regional and industry research for companiesFiat
ConsultingLong-term assignments and institutional advisoryFiat

Premium content and membership are excluded from the initial core utilities and are managed as future services (PLANNED). B2B Intelligence is expected to expand in Phase 3 as commercialization of North Korea NOW data progresses.
