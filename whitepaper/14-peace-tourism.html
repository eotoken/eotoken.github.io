Korean Peninsula Peace Tourism Belt

PUBLIC RELEASE · ENGLISH EDITION
STATUS
CONDITIONAL · Actual tourism projects proceed only if inter-Korean relations, government approvals, entry/exit rules, sanctions and licensing conditions are satisfied.

The Korean Peninsula Peace Tourism Belt is a long-term core concept that first accumulates tourism, cultural and ecological resources as content and data, then expands into tourism products, transportation, accommodation and events if institutional conditions permit.

VISION ROUTE
Seoul → DMZ → Kaesong → Pyongyang → Wonsan → Mt. Kumgang → Mt. Baekdu

The route above is a conceptual model for a long-term tourism belt, not a currently confirmed transportation or tourism route.

4-Stage Expansion Roadmap

1. Build tourism content and data - Korean Peninsula 100 Tourism Assets.

2. Develop joint tourism products - Actual products, reservation services and EO Utility integration.

3. Expand private investment and tourism infrastructure - Accommodation, transportation, resorts and events.

4. Build the Korean Peninsula Tourism Belt - A long-term network connecting major tourism hubs.

Korean Peninsula 100 Tourism Assets

The first stage of the Peace Tourism Belt is not infrastructure development but early construction of tourism content and data. The project aims to select 100 major tourism, cultural and ecological assets across both Koreas, systematically accumulate video, photography, historical, cultural and regional information, and study their potential for future tourism products.

Selection Framework

The intended model combines expert and public participation: tourism, history, culture, regional and North Korea specialists form an initial candidate pool, while selected input from Tongil TV viewers and the EO community may be incorporated.

Monetization Path

FREE CONTENT → PREMIUM DATA → B2B LICENSE → TOURISM PRODUCT
Basic content remains open, while deeper reports, data packs, video / photo licensing and future tourism products may provide additional monetization paths.

Although the Peace Tourism Belt as a whole is CONDITIONAL, preparation of content and data under the “Korean Peninsula 100 Tourism Assets” concept can be described as an advance-preparation area that may proceed earlier.

Rail, Hospitality & EO Utility

Rail & Sleeper Train

Rail and sleeper trains are emphasized as long-term transportation and experience elements of the Korean Peninsula Tourism Belt. The baseline model is not for the EO ecosystem to build or operate rail infrastructure directly; rather, EO aims to provide a tourism-product, content, reservation and Access Utility layer on infrastructure operated by governments, rail organizations and specialist operators.

Hospitality & Integrated Resort

Initially, the model is to work with specialist developers and hotel operators to connect tourism products, reservations, content and EO-exclusive benefits. In the longer term, project-level joint ventures or strategic equity participation may be considered only if commercial, legal and licensing conditions are satisfied. The public whitepaper describes this broadly as integrated tourism infrastructure combining accommodation, tourism and leisure rather than specifying unconfirmed facility types.

EO Utility in Tourism

EO payment availability designated by product.

Products supporting EO payment use either full EO payment or full fiat payment, not mixed payment.

Selected premium or limited tourism products may operate as EO-exclusive offerings.

Special schedules, seating, accommodation and experiences may connect to Experience & Access Utility.

EO use remains limited during the early content stage and expands as actual tourism products become available.
