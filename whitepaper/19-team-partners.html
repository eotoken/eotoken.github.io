PUBLIC RELEASE · ENGLISH EDITION

Core operators and advisers of EO Foundation, EO Global and Tongil TV are disclosed through official channels only to the extent that their roles, experience and responsibilities have been fact-checked and they have consented to public disclosure. Individuals not named in this whitepaper should not be regarded as officially disclosed.

Only partners with objectively verifiable contracts, MOUs or formal agreements are named. Institutions at the discussion or proposal stage are not presented as official partners.

PUBLIC DISCLOSURE STATUS
