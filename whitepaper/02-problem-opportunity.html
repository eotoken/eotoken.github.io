Problem & Opportunity

PUBLIC RELEASE · ENGLISH EDITION

The problems EO Token seeks to address can be organized into three layers. First, many digital-asset projects still lack repeatable real-world utility relative to trading and holding. Second, content, shopping, event and data services are often separated, fragmenting both user experience and business data. Third, media, regional and industrial information, education, commerce, tourism and long-term exchange services related to the Korean Peninsula are also fragmented.

1) Lack of Real-World Utility

EO places actual service use ahead of token trading itself. The objective is to answer the question, “Why should a user use EO?” inside real services such as shopping payments, event participation and information-product purchases.

2) Fragmented Content and Services

When media, commerce, events and data services are separated, user experience and business data become fragmented as well. The EO ecosystem aims to connect Media & Data, Commerce, Experience and Intelligence through a unified Utility Layer.

3) Fragmented Korean Peninsula Information and Services

Information related to North Korea, unification and the Korean Peninsula is dispersed across news, policy materials, research, video and separate online channels. Tongil TV's North Korea NOW is intended to transform this material from one-off news into accumulated content and data assets that can later support education, B2B services, tourism and commerce.

Opportunity: One Connected Ecosystem

EO's opportunity is not simply to list multiple businesses in parallel. The objective is to accumulate content as structured data, use that data to build trust and insight, build commerce, experience and information services on top of it, and use EO Token as the utility layer connecting those services.
