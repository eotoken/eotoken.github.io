EO Shopping Mall — Commerce Layer

PUBLIC RELEASE · ENGLISH EDITION
STATUS
PLANNED · Business and service structure under detailed development.

EO Shopping Mall is the commerce platform intended to implement EO Token's core Commerce Utility. The initial operating model centers on direct sales, with EO Global planning, sourcing and selling products directly. As operating experience and the user base expand, the model may develop into a Marketplace where external brands and sellers participate.

Product Strategy

Initial: Tongil TV / EO merchandise, content-linked products, general consumer goods and partner-brand products.

Expansion: Lawfully sourced Korean Peninsula traditional foods, cultural products and regionally themed products.

Long-term conditional: North Korea-related goods may be reviewed only where applicable law, sanctions, customs, inspection and country-of-origin requirements can be satisfied.

Payment Principle

A single order is paid either 100% in EO Token or 100% in fiat currency. Mixed EO-and-fiat payment within one order is not supported.

When EO payment is selected, the total order amount - including product price, shipping, and taxes that can be determined at the domestic point of payment - is converted into EO Token. Overseas taxes, duties and customs charges may vary by country and product.

EO Payment Pricing

EO payment pricing references market prices from a designated reference exchange.

The reference exchange may be designated or changed based on liquidity, price stability and service suitability.

Detailed price-calculation methodology and quote-validity periods are defined separately in Service Terms and the EO Technical Paper.

EO payment may be selectively restricted during extreme market moves, exchange outages or price-data errors.

Refund Principle

For ordinary goods paid in EO, refunds are generally based on the actual amount of EO paid by the user. Detailed refund periods, management of assets pending refund and settlement procedures are defined separately in EO Shopping Mall Service Terms and operating policies.
